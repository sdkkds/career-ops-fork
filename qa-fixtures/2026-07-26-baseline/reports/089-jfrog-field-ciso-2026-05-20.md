## C) Nivel y Estrategia

**JD level:** Executive IC -- $240-255K base + OTE implies Director/VP-equivalent scope. No management layer apparent.
**Candidate natural level:** Senior/Director -- has the depth but currently titles as "Senior PO." The 25-year span and CISSP close the level gap meaningfully.

### "Sell senior without lying" plan

Frame every story around the pattern: *advisory depth + executive communication + measurable program outcomes.*

1. **Lead with scope, not title:** "Built and ran security operations for Azure Government at Microsoft scale -- 10 to 45 engineers, 24/7/365, CJIS/DoD compliance, security incident command. That IS senior security leadership."
2. **Reframe NCC Group:** "Field advisory to CISO-level executives at Fortune 10/200 clients -- defined remediation roadmaps, ran cross-functional security programs, presented to C-suite stakeholders." Accurate and CISO-adjacent.
3. **Current role:** "Security product strategy owner for enterprise-wide offensive security program at a Fortune 100 health insurer." Accurate, senior scope.
4. **CISSP as signal:** Lean on it. Field CISO candidates at vendors routinely cite CISSP as the baseline credential.

**Cover letter anchor paragraph:**
> "Throughout my career -- from building Azure Government security operations at Microsoft to advising Fortune 10/200 CISOs on remediation programs at NCC Group -- I have operated at the intersection of technical security depth and executive stakeholder communication. The Field CISO role at JFrog is the natural next step: bringing that advisory experience to help enterprise customers build their DevSecOps practice around a platform I respect."

**Compensation strategy:** $240-255K base is stated. Do not anchor below it. If asked for expectations, respond: "I'm targeting roles in the $240-255K base range, consistent with what JFrog has posted. Happy to discuss total comp structure including OTE and equity."
