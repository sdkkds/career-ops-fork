## C) Nivel y Estrategia

**Nivel detectado:** Staff IC — equivalent to Principal/Senior Manager. Huntress is building their PM org; Staff PM works directly with VP of Product and assists CTO. High-autonomy, high-ownership role.

**Candidate's natural level:** Senior PM / IT Product Owner. The Staff title is one level above where he is now. Not an extreme stretch, but meaningful.

### Plan "Vender Senior Sin Mentir"

1. **Lead with defender credibility, not builder:** "I've been on the other side of every SIEM decision — managing security incidents for 5M+ endpoints at AzureGov scale, coordinating pen tests for Fortune 10 clients. I know exactly what's broken about enterprise SIEMs for resource-constrained teams because I lived it."

2. **Own the SMB translation angle:** Huntress's thesis is that SIEM doesn't work for SMBs because it's overbuilt. Your entire current role is translating enterprise security practices to a resource-limited team at a health insurer. That's the bridge.

3. **Reframe PM tenure:** 5 years formal PM (Adisys Apr 2022 → now). Before that: 9 years program management at Microsoft vendors, engagement management at NCC Group. "I've been running product-shaped programs for 12 years; the title caught up in 2022."

4. **CISSP as differentiator:** Most SaaS PMs don't hold active CISSP. Position as: "I can be in a threat-intelligence discussion with SOC engineers in the morning and a pricing strategy meeting with sales in the afternoon — and be credible in both rooms."

5. **POPM certification (2026):** Fresh, directly relevant — shows intentional upskill toward this trajectory.

### Plan "Si Me Downlevelan"
- Accept Senior PM title if base stays at $200K+
- Negotiate for Staff title review at 6 months with clear success metrics (SIEM feature adoption, customer NPS on SIEM)
- Walk-away: $185K base (given other strong options in pipeline)
