## C) Nivel y Estrategia

**Nivel en JD:** Senior (5+ years)
**Nivel natural de Sunjay:** Comfortably senior — 25 years total, 5 years in cybersecurity-specific PM/PO roles.

This role is likely a slight step down in title from current Sr. IT PO trajectory, but appropriate for a lateral move into an industry-branded SaaS TPM role that builds toward Director/Principal.

### Plan "Sell Senior Without Lying"

- Lead with **Fortune 10/200 security program scale at NCC Group** — most candidates applying to this role have never run pentest programs at that client tier.
- Lead with **exceeding penetration testing completion targets** two years running at health insurance — quantifiable program management output.
- **Reframe healthcare as regulated SaaS-adjacent**: HIPAA/enterprise compliance environment is arguably more stringent than many SaaS companies.
- **CISSP + POPM combo** signals both technical credibility and product/program maturity — rare in TPM candidates.
- Position **demand intake at scale (100+ annual engagements)** as the exact operational muscle the JD describes.

Key phrase: *"I've managed the full lifecycle of external penetration test programs at Fortune 10 clients — scoping, vendor coordination, technical conversations with engineers on findings, and executive stakeholder reporting. That's exactly what this role asks for."*

### Plan "If Downleveled"

- Accept if: base ≥ $155K (gets to acceptable total comp with bonus/equity); clear path to Principal/Staff TPM within 18 months; review at 6 months with explicit performance criteria.
- Criteria for 6-month review: own at least one cross-functional security program from 0→1; KPI reporting established for security team.
- Decline if: base < $150K or role scoped as "coordinator" rather than program driver.
