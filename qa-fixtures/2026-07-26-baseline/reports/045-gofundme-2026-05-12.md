### Level Assessment
- **JD level:** Staff TPM — senior IC contributor, org-wide scope, reports to CISO/VP Security
- **Sunjay's natural level:** Senior/Principal PM — current role is senior IC with program ownership, but title is "Senior IT Product Owner"
- **Gap:** Title gap, not scope gap. The substance of current + prior roles (Fortune 10 programs, bi-coastal infrastructure build, parallel engagement management) fully supports Staff-level claims.

### Plan: Sell Senior Without Lying

1. **Lead with scope, not title.** "As the product owner for a 6-person Offensive Security Red Team at a healthcare enterprise, I drive the full program lifecycle — backlog, metrics, executive reporting, and cross-functional stakeholder alignment — while managing 100+ annual engagements."
2. **The Fortune 10 client is your Staff-level proof.** NCC Group's largest North American client = Fortune 10. That's Staff-level stakes. Use it explicitly.
3. **25 years as accelerator, not liability.** Framed correctly: "I've been doing this since before TPM was a job title — built the programs, built the teams, built the metrics."
4. **CISSP + POPM as differentiator.** Most TPMs don't have CISSP. Most security leaders don't have SAFe POPM. The combination is rare.
5. **Healthcare context as bonus.** GoFundMe handles financial data (PCI/PII sensitivity). Your health insurance context shows you operate in a regulated, sensitive-data environment.

### Plan: If Downleveled to Senior TPM

- Accept if base comp ≥ $150K and there's a clear review window (6 months)
- Criteria for reconsideration: expanded program scope, CISO visibility, equity refresh
- Ask explicitly: "What does the promotion path to Staff look like, and what would that milestone be?"
