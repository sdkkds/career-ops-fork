**Gap 2: Board-level security reporting** *(Required for C-suite)*
- Hard blocker? Moderate — documented VP/Director-level exec comms; board-level is one step up.
- Adjacent: At a small company like MIE, "board" likely means 5–8 people. Not a Fortune 500 board. Existing executive reporting experience maps closely.
- Mitigation: Frame "VP and Director stakeholder communications" as equivalent scope. Small-company boards don't expect polished enterprise-CISO board decks on day one.

**Gap 3: No prior CISO title** *(Structural)*
- Hard blocker? Moderate — screeners often filter on title. But MIE cannot afford a seasoned healthcare CISO (those cost $250k–$350k+ base).
- Adjacent: Functional CISO-equivalent activities: security program ownership (current), penetration testing program management (NCC Group + current), enterprise security remediation (NCC Group).
- Mitigation: Cover letter should address directly. "You need someone who can build this program, not someone who maintains an established one. That's been my career."

**Gap 4: Specific tool stack (Rapid7, SentinelOne, KnowBe4)** *(Learnable)*
- Hard blocker? No — all widely deployed; none require years of ramp.
- Mitigation: Acknowledge gaps honestly; note comparable tools in current stack (Jira/ADO for tracking; Azure Defender/Sentinel adjacent to SentinelOne; familiarity with security awareness program management).
