- Strongest phrases: "security-native PM," "buyer-turned-builder," "25 years on the customer side"

**Honest assessment:** The better play is to target B2B SaaS security product companies that are hiring PMs at the PM (not Senior PM) level for a genuine first product company role, build the track record for 12–18 months, then apply to Senior PM roles at Abnormal's tier.
