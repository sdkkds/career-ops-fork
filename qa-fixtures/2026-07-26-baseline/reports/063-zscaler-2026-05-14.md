**Candidate's natural level for this archetype:** Senior PM / PM II at a security vendor — needs 1–2 years of vendor PM experience before Principal is realistic at Zscaler scale.

**Gap between roles:** The archetype mismatch is material. Candidate is an excellent security program manager and enterprise IT product owner. This role buys a vendor product manager who deeply understands ZTNA, can do customer discovery with enterprise buyers (as a seller/vendor, not as internal customer), define product features for a commercial SaaS product, and drive GTM. These are related but distinct skills.

**If asked to interview anyway:** Lead with security domain expertise (CISSP, penetration testing program ownership, AzureGov), frame PO experience as "product strategy for internal security platform," prepare STAR stories on stakeholder alignment and cross-functional delivery. Be honest about the vendor PM gap — they will probe it.
