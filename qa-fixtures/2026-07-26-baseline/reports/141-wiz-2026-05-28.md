### Nivel detectado
- **JD level:** Head of TPM / Director equivalent (~L7-L8). Reports to CISO/CIO, manages a team of TPMs, owns org-level roadmap.
- **Candidate natural level:** Senior/Principal (~L6). Senior IT Product Owner + prior Senior TPM. Strong pedigree but ~1-2 level gap.

### Plan "vender senior sin mentir"

The pitch is NOT "I've done this exact job." The pitch is: **"I'm the operator who built programs at the scale this role requires, and I'm ready to stop being the implementer and start being the leader of implementers."**

Specific framing angles:
- **Scale signal:** "Built a 45-person bi-coastal engineering team from scratch at Microsoft running 24/7/365 Azure Government infrastructure. I know what it takes to build, develop, and hold a team together."
- **Security PM credibility:** "Coordinated parallel Fortune 10 penetration testing programs and owned the security remediation verification program for NCC Group's largest North American client. That's exactly the cross-org complexity this role describes."
- **Player-coach fit:** "I'm currently a player-coach: product owner for a 6-person Red Team in a $[large] health insurance org. I own the backlog AND coordinate with Engineering, Legal, and business units simultaneously."
- **Trajectory signal:** "CISSP + 25 years across Microsoft, NCC Group, and healthcare security — I have both the technical depth and the executive-communication experience to operate at the CISO/CIO level from day one."

### Plan "si me downlevelan"
This role is unlikely to downlevel — it's a "Head of" with explicit team management responsibilities. If offered a Senior TPM IC role instead:
- Accept ONLY if: TC ≥ $200K, explicit written path to Head promotion within 12 months with clear criteria
- Ask: "Is there a roadmap to the Head role? What would a 6-month review look like?"
