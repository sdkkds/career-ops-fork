## Block B — North Star Alignment: 1.5/5

This role doesn't map to any of the six target archetypes. It's a hands-on practitioner position — the kind of work Sunjay's Red Team *does*, not the kind of work Sunjay *does*. The role calls for someone writing detection signatures, triaging alerts, and running threat hunts. That's the operator layer, not the product/program/consulting layer.

Anduril is also a defense technology company building autonomous systems for the military. That's a distinct industry with a distinct culture — fast, mission-driven, deeply engineering-oriented. The target industries are healthcare, big tech, financial services, legal, and security consulting. Defense is not on the list, and it's a meaningful cultural departure.

Career capital: working at Anduril builds a specialized detection engineering skillset, not PM/leadership credentials. Would not accelerate the vCISO or consulting practice trajectory.
