**Nivel detectado in JD:** Principal (reports to Director; owns significant roadmap portion; cross-functional leadership across world-class teams)

**Candidate's natural level:** Senior IT Product Owner / Program Manager — internal IT organization, not commercial product management

**The core mismatch:** "Principal Product Manager" at a security vendor means you've launched products that customers buy. It means quarterly roadmap reviews with enterprise customers, pricing/packaging decisions, GTM strategy with Sales, and shipping features to thousands of paying clients. Sunjay's experience is owning delivery for an internal 6-person red team. These are genuinely different functions.

**Plan "sell senior without lying":**
- Emphasize cross-functional leadership at Microsoft scale (bi-coastal 45-person team)
- Frame NCC Group as "engaged Fortune 10/200 enterprise security buyers at scale, translating complex security assessment requirements into operational programs"
- Azure Government infra ownership = "deep enterprise cloud architecture operations knowledge"
- Red Team PO = "product ownership with full security domain depth, managing backlog, user stories, and delivery for a specialized security capability"

**Reality check:** Any PM interview at Zscaler will include product scenario questions (prioritization, GTM, customer discovery, roadmapping). The gap between internal IT PO and commercial security PM is wide and would be immediately apparent. This isn't a framing problem — it's a genuine experience gap that can't be bridged with language.
