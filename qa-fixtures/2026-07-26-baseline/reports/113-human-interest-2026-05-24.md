Frame it: "built security guardrails into Azure Government infrastructure for federal clients with DoD/CJIS compliance before 'shift-left security' was a common phrase."

### Plan "si me downlevelan"

If offered at a mid-level TPM rate: accept only if total comp stays ≥ $185K base + equity with a clear 6-month review and defined Senior TPM criteria. The $200–220K range is the posted senior range — anchor there and negotiate to stay in it.
