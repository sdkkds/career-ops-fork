### Gaps — Mitigation Strategy

**Gap 1: No B2B commercial product management experience** (Medium-High Blocker)
- *Hard blocker?* No — adjacent roles with internal PO have crossed over before
- *Adjacent experience:* Owns product roadmap for an internal security service (offensive security); manages user stories and features for internal "customers" (development teams); formal SAFe POPM certification
- *Mitigation:* Reframe "product ownership for internal security programs" as "building and managing a security product that serves 10+ internal customer teams at a major health insurer" — the mechanics are the same; the customer is internal vs. external
- *Portfolio gap:* No public product case studies; could address in cover letter

**Gap 2: No Application Control / ESPM domain knowledge** (High Blocker for technical depth)
- *Hard blocker?* Yes for depth, but the JD says "Experience using or building" — "using" is a lower bar
- *Adjacent experience:* Sysadmin background (AppLocker, Group Policy, endpoint config) and offensive security work (knows application control from the attacker/evasion perspective)
- *Mitigation:* Frame Microsoft sysadmin experience as "I lived on the endpoint side — I know what good application control looks like operationally" + study the vendor landscape (Carbon Black App Control, Ivanti, Airlock, CrowdStrike App Control) before any screening call

**Gap 3: 6+ years PM in cybersecurity** (Minor — borderline)
- Formally: ~5 years PM/PO in cybersecurity. NCC Group PM (2021–2023) + current PO (2023–present) = ~5 years
- Pre-PO: Penetration testing coordinator managing 100+ engagements/year — arguably PM-adjacent
- *Mitigation:* Count coordinator role as early-career PM; total security-domain PM + PM-adjacent = 6+ years
