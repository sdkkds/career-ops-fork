## C) Nivel y Estrategia

### Nivel

- **JD targets:** Director (7+ yrs infosec, 3+ leadership, security program ownership)
- **Sunjay's natural level:** Senior/Principal PM → adjacent to Director; never held a Director title but has acted as program owner and team leader at Director scope
- **Assessment:** Legitimate stretch — not overreach. The communication and leadership skills are there. The gap is having the "Director" title on the resume, not having the capability.

### Plan "Sell Senior Without Lying"

1. **Lead with CISSP + scale.** "CISSP with 25+ years in enterprise security programs" lands above most candidates for a Director-level role at a startup.
2. **Reframe Product Owner → Security Program Director.** The PO role at health insurance IS program ownership — you set strategy, prioritize the roadmap, manage executive stakeholders. Frame it: "Director of Offensive Security Program" equivalence.
3. **NCC Group = startup-adjacent.** Consulting at Fortune 10/200 scale = high-pressure, fast-moving, client-facing. This is more relevant to startup agility than another big enterprise role.
4. **Customer-facing security = direct JD hit.** Jasper needs someone to "jump on calls with prospective enterprise customers." This is the #1 differentiator from a pure internal security leader.
5. **Microsoft at scale.** "24/7/365 Azure Government infrastructure for federal clients" demonstrates enterprise-grade execution, not bureaucratic slowdown.
6. **Founder framing for ERG.** Founded and chaired Employee Mental Wellbeing ERG = startup initiative skills.

### Plan "If Downleveled"

- If offered Senior Security Manager vs. Director: accept IF comp stays at $216K+ floor and there's a 6-month path to Director title with clear criteria in writing.
- Comp is the primary driver here — don't sacrifice $216K base for title alone.
