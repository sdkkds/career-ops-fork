- "25+ years across Microsoft-scale infrastructure and Fortune 10/200 security consulting — I don't need to learn what enterprise security risk looks like, I've been living it."

- On the "Director" gap: "As Product Owner for Offensive Security, I own the program roadmap, align with the CISO and executive team on priorities, and translate threat intelligence into product decisions. The scope is Director-level responsibility without the title."
- On vCISO: "I'm actively building my consulting practice. DYOPATH's vision of embedding fractional CISO advisory into your service offering is exactly what I want to build at scale."
- **Founder-as-advantage:** Independent consulting practice → proof of ability to sell, deliver, and iterate security advisory without institutional support.

### Plan "Si Me Downlevelan"

If DYOPATH tries to hire at Senior Manager vs Director:
- Accept only if: base ≥ $150K, clear 6-month path to Director with defined criteria, and title/scope includes client-facing vCISO work.
- Non-negotiable: Remote status.
- Request written 6-month review criteria in offer letter.
