### Gaps y Mitigación

**Gap 1: No banking/financial institution experience (HARD BLOCKER)**
- Hard blocker or nice-to-have? **Hard blocker.** JD says "at least 5 years in leadership capacity at a regulated financial institution or Fintech." Health insurance is not a financial institution under FDIC/FFIEC. The gap is structural.
- Adjacent experience? Healthcare is regulated (HIPAA) but a completely different regulatory framework. Not bridgeable.
- Portfolio project? No.
- Mitigation: None viable. This is a first-pass screen killer. Recruiters filling a bank CISO role will filter on "banking/fintech" immediately.

**Gap 2: No FDIC/FFIEC compliance experience (HARD BLOCKER)**
- Hard blocker or nice-to-have? **Hard blocker.** The entire de novo bank thesis depends on FDIC examination readiness. FIL-66-2019, FIL-13-2021, Interagency Guidelines — these require specific prior exposure.
- Adjacent experience? AzureGov (CJIS/DoD) shows regulated-environment familiarity but is not transferable to FDIC banking context.
- Mitigation: Could study/certify (CISA, CRISC, banking security certs), but that's a 12-18 month runway, not a near-term play.

**Gap 3: No CISO-level track record (SIGNIFICANT)**
- Currently Senior IT Product Owner. This is an Executive Leadership Team role at a bank.
- Two title levels up. The jump from Sr. Product Owner to Bank CISO is steep even with 25 years total experience.
- The de novo context makes it worse: they need someone proven, not someone building their first CISO track record at a bank they're launching.
- Mitigation: Frame vCISO consulting practice, but without actual bank CISO tenure this is a stretch.

**Gap 4: Third-party risk program ownership (MODERATE)**
- NCC Group client management is adjacent but not formal TPRM program design.
- Banks have strict vendor risk management requirements (OCC, FDIC guidance).
- Mitigation: Bridgeable in narrative, but if screened against hard criteria it's weak.

**Gap 5: Defensive threat monitoring / SOC (MODERATE)**
- Background is primarily offensive security (red team, pentesting). No SOC leadership, no threat detection program ownership.
- Banks need defensive monitoring capability (threat intel, SIEM, detection engineering).
- Mitigation: Can frame incident management + program oversight, but technical depth is on offensive not defensive side.
