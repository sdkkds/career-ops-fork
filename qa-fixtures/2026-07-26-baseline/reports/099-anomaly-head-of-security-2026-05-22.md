## C) Nivel y Estrategia

**Nivel detectado:** Solo contributor with full program ownership → CISO growth track. Effectively a Director/Head of Security title at startup scale. Sunjay's natural level is Senior IC / Director — this aligns.

**Gap from "natural level":** Sunjay has operated as a senior PM/PO *within* security programs at large orgs. This role requires him to *own* the program without a security org above him. That's a real step up in accountability — but not a technical leap.

**Plan "vender senior sin mentir":**

1. **Lead with the healthcare domain match.** Anomaly sells to providers; Sunjay works at a payer. He understands both sides of the denial/payment dynamic. This is a rare combination — most security candidates won't have this specific knowledge.

2. **Frame Fortune 10/200 scale as a superpower.** "I've seen what world-class security programs look like at organizations 1,000x your size. I know what to build now and what to defer until you need it." This directly addresses the startup-vs-enterprise gap.

3. **CISSP + POPM as credibility combo.** Most security leaders at this stage lack formal product ownership methodology. Sunjay can build the ISMS with SAFe discipline — measurable, backlog-driven, iterative. Differentiated framing.

4. **Emphasize the breadth of NCC Group experience.** Coordinating multiple concurrent pen tests across Fortune 10/200 clients = deep exposure to how large orgs run security programs and respond to findings. Translate this into: "I've been inside more security programs in 18 months at NCC Group than most people see in a career."

**Plan "si me downlevelan":**
- "Head of Security" without CISO title is fine at this company size. The JD explicitly says the role "may evolve into a dedicated CISO position."
- Accept the role if: base ≥ $180K, equity ≥ 0.15%, clear CISO succession language in offer letter.
- Request 6-month review with written criteria for CISO elevation.
- Walk away if: base < $170K, no equity conversation, title has "Manager" not "Head/Director."
